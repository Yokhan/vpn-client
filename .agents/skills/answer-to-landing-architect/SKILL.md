# Answer To Landing Architect

Turn content into a decision flow.

Output contract:

- Landing goal.
- Reader paths.
- Content inventory.
- Sections.
- Interactions.
- Glossary terms.
- Data model.
- QA requirements.

Default sections:

1. Hero with verdict.
2. Fast path.
3. Scenario selector.
4. Decision matrix.
5. Detailed cards.
6. Checker or readiness checklist.
7. Glossary.
8. Copyable templates.
9. Final recommendation.

Do not merely typeset the source text.
