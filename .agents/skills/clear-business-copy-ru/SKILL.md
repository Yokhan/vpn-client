# Clear Business Copy RU

Copy rules:

- Start with the decision or benefit.
- One paragraph means one thought.
- Prefer verbs over abstract nouns.
- Explain consequences, not just features.
- Use specific labels: `Проверить готовность`, not `Подробнее`.
- Cut filler and inflated claims.
- Keep warnings firm but not insulting.

Remove UI meta-text:

- `я проверил`
- `в этом ответе`
- `как я писал выше`
- `пользователь попросил`
- `можно докрутить`
- `canvas`
- chat/service comments
