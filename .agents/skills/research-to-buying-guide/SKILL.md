# Research To Buying Guide

Use for purchase comparisons.

Required sections:

- Verdict.
- Use cases.
- Ranking cards.
- Deal checker.
- Test plan.
- Seller messages.
- Glossary.

If prices, laws, availability, or product revisions may have changed, verify current information before finalizing.
